# LiftChase app icon alternatives

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as 1024 x 1024 opaque PNG masters.

## Shared prompt direction

- iOS app icon for LiftChase, a shared strength-workout logging app.
- Minimal vector-like geometry with one strong focal point and a bold silhouette.
- Deep navy edge-to-edge background with electric coral as the dominant symbol color and restrained mint accents.
- Legible at 40–60 px and recognizable in grayscale.
- No text, letters, numbers, watermark, device mockup, photographic texture, tiny details, thin lines, or baked rounded corners.
- Use the shipping coral barbell/progress icon only as a brand reference; create a genuinely different original mark.

## Concept prompts

1. **Partner Plates** — Two bold interlocking circular weight plates form one unified strength symbol, with a subtle upward notch in the negative space suggesting progress and training together.
2. **Completed Sets** — Three large rounded completion tiles rise diagonally like a progress staircase, crossed or crowned by a compact barbell silhouette; one mint tile marks completion.
3. **Lift Loop** — One continuous thick coral ribbon forms a compact barbell and loops through itself, suggesting repeated workouts, continuity, and long-term progress without using a separate arrowhead.
4. **Shared Rack** — Two mirrored coral support shapes rise from the lower corners to cradle one mint barbell, conveying partners spotting and training together through abstract geometry.
5. **Progress Plate** — One bold coral weight plate with three radial cutouts; the central negative space contains a restrained mint rising pulse or mountain peak suggesting measurable progress.
