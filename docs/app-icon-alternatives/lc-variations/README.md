# Lift Chase LC icon variations

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as 1024 x 1024 opaque PNG masters. The Completed Sets and Lift Loop alternatives were used as palette, geometry, spacing, and polish references.

## Shared direction

- Create an iOS app icon for Lift Chase, a shared strength-workout logging app.
- Make `L` and `C` emerge from functional barbell, plate, workout-set, motion-path, or negative-space geometry instead of placing ordinary typed initials on the icon.
- Preserve the deep navy background and electric orange/coral primary mark, with teal used sparingly as a completion or barbell accent.
- Use minimal vector-like geometry, thick forms, one centered focal emblem, a strong silhouette, and generous safe margins.
- Keep the mark recognizable at 40–60 px and in grayscale.
- No words, numbers, watermark, device mockup, photographic texture, tiny details, thin lines, or baked rounded corners.

## Concept prompts

1. **LC Lift Path** — A continuous orange path becomes a barbell: the left weight and lower bar form an `L`, while the center path curves into a broad `C`. The initials appear on a second look after the strength/progress symbol.
2. **Loaded C** — A dominant open `C`-shaped weight plate surrounds a teal bar and orange upright that form an `L`-shaped lifting rack. Both initials are functional gym geometry.
3. **LC Set Stack** — Rounded workout-set tiles stack into an abstract orange `L`, while a curved teal completion tile and barbell end imply a `C`. The mark combines logged sets with strength progress.
4. **Chased Loop** — One thick orange lift path begins as an `L`, then curls into a near-circular `C` around a compact teal barbell hub. The path suggests a repeatable training cycle.
5. **Negative-Space Load** — A slightly rising orange barbell uses its left plate and bar to form an `L`, its right weight geometry to form a `C`, and a teal center collar to suggest a completed set.
