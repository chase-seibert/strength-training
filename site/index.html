Made for the way you train

The same workout can belong to everyone.

A parent and kid. A couple. You and a training partner. Pick who’s joining today, then log everyone’s work in one place, with individual numbers and progress.

01 / Train together

One routine.
Each person gets their own numbers.

Log loads, reps, and completed sets side by side for up to three people. Choose a smaller crew whenever you train solo or as a pair.

Dad Mom Mia
02 / Ready when you are

Your workout doesn’t wait for Wi‑Fi.

Core routine setup, workout logging, history, and the exercise library are on your iPhone. No login screen, connection check, or cloud sync needed to get through a session.

03 / Find your next movement

A big library, already in your pocket.

Browse 873 exercises with equipment, muscle, and form guidance. The full catalog is on your iPhone, so you don’t need to be online to find your next movement.

Log it together.
Keep progress separate.

Their exercise, right on their wrist.
